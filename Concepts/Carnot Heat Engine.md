# Carnot Heat Engine

## Definition
A Carnot engine is an idealized heat engine that operates on the reversible Carnot cycle.

## Maximum Efficiency
The Carnot efficiency is given by:
$$\eta = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}$$

## Related Concepts
Fundamentally connected to [[Thermodynamics Fundamentals]] and the second law of thermodynamics governing [[Entropy]].
